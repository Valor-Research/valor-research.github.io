About

I am a fixed-income portfolio manager by training and by trade, with a doctorate in quantitative finance, and these pages are the small public corner of a larger private hobby project: reading the government bond markets carefully, every day, from the sources that publish them. The curves here are redrawn each morning from the parameters the Bundesbank and the Federal Reserve Board release; the weekly letter says what moved in the Treasury and Bund markets, by how much and against which published series, in plain sentences, with every figure taken from the public source named beside it. It is reporting, not advice: there is no rating, no target, no recommendation, and nothing here takes account of any reader's circumstances. I hold positions and trade for my own account, and the pages never say which. The site is free and carries no advertising; if it is useful to you, there is a way to support it, and nothing changes for readers who do not. If a figure looks wrong or a source misread, write to me - corrections are made in the open, and a corrected issue says what was corrected.
