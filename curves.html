The last year of curves

Far forward rates sit below where short rates are expected to be partly because of convexity: a long bond's price rises more when yields fall than it drops when they rise, so investors accept a lower yield for it, and the forward curve bends down at its long end - the published curves carry this already.

One curve a week: the last published day of each week, the Bundesbank's and the Federal Reserve Board's zero-coupon curves.
